## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a clinical, crisp, and distraction-free light mode environment engineered for local-first desktop and mobile productivity workspaces. Grounded in a high-contrast minimalist philosophy and guided by conservative Material 3 Expressive fundamentals, the visual language prioritizes deep cognitive focus, typographic cadence, and instantaneous spatial orientation.

Key qualities define the brand posture:
- **Atmospheric Purity**: Pure, warm-tinted off-white surfaces (`#F8FAFC`, `#FFFFFF`) eliminate glare while avoiding the sterile severity of unmodulated gray.
- **Intentional Focus Accents**: A vibrant Electric Indigo signature draws immediate focus to execution states, active timers, and primary operations without sensory overload.
- **Architectural Utility**: Distinctive tabular numerals and structural headings set in Space Grotesk are balanced against the neutral, legible clarity of Inter for dense information cards and checklists.
- **Local-First Tactility**: Discrete hairline boundaries, solid state indicators, and deterministic visual transitions communicate rock-solid reliability and speed.

## Colors

The light mode color system enforces an uncompromising functional hierarchy where high-contrast neutrals structure the interface and chromatic saturation is reserved strictly for interaction, timer execution, and task state.

### Core Canvas & Neutral Palette
- `surface-canvas` (`#F8FAFC`): The ambient root background canvas, calibrated to reduce eye strain while remaining crisp.
- `surface-card` (`#FFFFFF`): Elevated pure-white containers that provide immediate separation for interactive cards and lists.
- `surface-container` (`#F1F5F9`): Soft neutral fill for input fields, chip baselines, and nested schedule blocks.
- `surface-border` (`#E2E8F0`): Clean structural perimeter lines defining component edges without heavy shadow casting.
- `text-primary` (`#0F172A`): Deep slate text providing high WCAG AAA contrast for body and headers.
- `text-muted` (`#64748B`): Secondary labels, keyboard hints, and inactive timestamps.

### Brand & Interactive Colors
- `primary` (`#4F46E5`): Deep Electric Indigo for primary operational buttons, active progress tracks, and current timeblock indicators.
- `secondary` (`#6366F1`): Lighter indigo tone for interactive hover states, active badges, and multi-select highlights.

### Semantic Status Colors
- **Success / Done** (`#10B981`): Task completion, cloud sync confirmation, and valid inputs.
- **Attention / Medium Priority** (`#F59E0B`): Pending deadlines, pause states, and medium-urgency tasks.
- **Destructive / Error** (`#EF4444`): Overdue tasks, deletion warnings, and validation failures.
- **Session Types**: Focus mode leverages `#4F46E5`, Short Break shifts to Teal (`#0D9488`), and Long Break resolves to Violet (`#7C3AED`).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the technical character of Space Grotesk with the systematic utility of Inter.

### Role Assignments
- **Countdown Display & Metric Counters (`Space Grotesk`)**: `display-lg` and `display-md` are strictly reserved for focus countdown readouts and numerical milestone stats. They must enforce tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent layout jump during live ticking.
- **Section Headers (`Space Grotesk`)**: `headline-lg` and `headline-md` establish spatial boundaries for workspace views, analytics breakdowns, and session transitions.
- **Workflow & Task Hierarchy (`Inter`)**: `title-md` and `title-sm` anchor actionable item titles. `body-md` and `body-sm` provide effortless reading for notes, metadata, and subtasks.
- **Interactive Controls & Chips (`Inter`)**: `label-md` and `label-sm` govern button text, status tokens, and shortcut markers with slightly expanded tracking to maintain legibility at small point sizes.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is anchored to an uncompromising 8pt spatial grid with a 4pt micro-step for tight structural alignments.

### Grid & Density Rules
- **Margins & Gutters**: Mobile views start with a compact `1rem` (16px) margin and gutter, stepping up to `1.5rem` on tablet viewports and `2rem` on desktop workstations.
- **Vertical Rhythm**: Containers, task items, and split panels rely on `space-md` (16px) and `space-lg` (24px) vertical margins to separate distinct thought domains without cluttering the screen.
- **Touch & Click Boundaries**: Regardless of visual footprint, all primary interactive nodes (checkboxes, icon actions, segmented indicators) adhere strictly to a minimum target envelope of 48px × 48px.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system deliberately eschews deep, blurry drop shadows, skeuomorphic bevels, and frosted glass treatments. Spatial hierarchy is communicated through high-clarity surface tiers combined with 1px structural outlines.

### Elevation Hierarchy
1. **Level 0 (Canvas Base - `#F8FAFC`)**: The screen backdrop upon which all layout grids are positioned.
2. **Level 1 (Card & Module Tier - `#FFFFFF`)**: Task items, schedule timelines, and control panels sit cleanly on crisp white tiles bordered by a hairline stroke (`1px solid #E2E8F0`).
3. **Level 2 (Active / Floating Overlays)**: Dialogs, context dropdowns, and flyout datepickers utilize `#FFFFFF` coupled with a restrained, high-diffuse micro-shadow to separate them from the content beneath:
   - `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04);`
   - Border: `1px solid #E2E8F0`

## Shapes

The shape hierarchy establishes consistent structure through uniform 8px base radiuses (`roundedness: 2`), reflecting the ROUND_EIGHT aesthetic foundation.

- **Base Components (8px / `rounded`)**: Form inputs, buttons, checklist rows, schedule blocks, and modal window perimeters.
- **Card Containers (16px / `rounded-lg`)**: Primary structural panels, timeblock grouping envelopes, and session summary cards.
- **Pill Indicators (Full / 9999px)**: Status tags, priority chips, focus timer rings, and segmented control sliders.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action Button**: Solid `#4F46E5` fill with pure `#FFFFFF` label text, 8px radius, height of 40px (desktop) or 48px (mobile). Hover state deepens to `#4338CA`.
- **Secondary / Outlined Button**: Transparent or `#FFFFFF` fill with `1px solid #E2E8F0`, label in `text-primary` (`#0F172A`). Hover state applies `#F1F5F9`.
- **Text / Ghost Button**: No border, transparent background, text in `text-secondary` (`#475569`). Focus state highlights with an indigo ring.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status & Priority Chips**: Pill geometry (`rounded: 9999px`), 28px height.
  - Background: Soft 12% tint of the respective semantic color (e.g., `#ECFDF5` for Done, `#FEF3C7` for Medium Priority).
  - Border: 1px outline in corresponding semantic color at 25% opacity.
  - Content: 12px status dot or icon paired with `label-sm` text.

### Task & Schedule Cards
- Surface: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with `1px solid #E2E8F0` border and 8px border radius.
- Padding: `space-md` (16px) uniform internal spacing.
- Overdue State: Displays a 3px vertical accent border along the left edge using `#EF4444`.
- Active Focus Block: Perimeter border transitions to `1.5px solid #4F46E5` with a subtle `#EEF2FF` surface fill.

### Input Fields & Controls
- Surface: `#F1F5F9` resting fill, switching to `#FFFFFF` on active focus.
- Resting Border: `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- Focus Border: `1.5px solid #4F46E5` with zero offset glow.
- Checkboxes & Radios: 18px × 18px box with 4px corner radius. Unchecked state uses `#FFFFFF` fill and `#CBD5E1` border; checked state fills with `#4F46E5` displaying a crisp white checkmark glyph.

### Timer Ring & Displays
- Track: 8px stroke in `#E2E8F0`.
- Active Progress Arc: 8px stroke in `#4F46E5` (or mode-specific session color).
- Readout: Centered `display-lg` Space Grotesk tabular digits in `#0F172A`, framed above a sub-label in `label-md` `#64748B`.
